# Alife Health

> Alife Health is an AI-powered fertility technology company building clinical decision-support tools for IVF clinics and fertility patients. Its Alife Assist platform integrates with clinic EMR systems to provide data-driven insights across the IVF cycle. Alife is a portfolio company of Union Square Ventures. No public API, developer portal, or SDK is currently published.

## Products
- [Platform Overview](https://www.alifehealth.com/platform/): Alife Assist clinical platform for IVF clinics, integrating with EMR systems.
- [Embryo Predict](https://www.alifehealth.com/embryo-predict/): AI tool to assist embryo assessment and transfer decisions for Day 5-7 blastocysts.
- [Schedule Predict](https://www.alifehealth.com/schedule-predict/): Optimizes egg-retrieval scheduling to reduce lab overcapacity.
- [Success Predictor](https://www.alifehealth.com/success-predictor/): Patient success-likelihood modeling to support conversion.
- [Alife App](https://www.alifehealth.com/alife-app/): Patient-facing IVF companion application.
